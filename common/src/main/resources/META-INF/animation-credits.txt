Glaive animation acknowledgments

Better Combat by Zsolt Molnar (daedelus) and contributors:
https://github.com/ZsoltMolnarrr/BetterCombat

Simply Swords by Sweeney / Timefall Development and contributors:
https://github.com/Sweenus/SimplySwords

Simply Swords' use of Better Combat informed an earlier two-handed animation
prototype. That prototype has been replaced by native trident-style resting
placement and an original fixed-grip, shoulder-driven aiming implementation.

Essence Ascendance's glaive aiming, interpolation and rendering code are
original implementations. No Better Combat or Simply Swords source code,
keyframe data, models, textures or sounds are included. Neither mod nor an
external player-animation library is required or bundled.
