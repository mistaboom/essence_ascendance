# Essence Ascendance 1.0.0-beta.4

For Minecraft 1.21.1, on Fabric and NeoForge.

## Ascendance fishing rod

- Added the Ascendance fishing rod with all six equipment tiers, tier-colored rod and hook artwork, Infuser progression, and Archive guidance.
- Water casts use ordinary fishing, including native loot, open-water checks, Lure, and Luck of the Sea. Fishing Speed improves the native fishing countdowns.
- Hook blocks to pull yourself toward the anchor, or hook supported entities to pull them toward you. Start pulling with a strong hook-setting tug, steer the opening burst by looking sideways, up, or down, then keep holding for steady reeling. Release to detach while retaining momentum.
- Improved close-range pulls so hooked targets settle in front of the caster without passing them and rebounding away.
- Shortened rod tooltips into separate range, hook speed, hook gravity, pull force, and reel speed lines.
- Surface Tension supports exposed liquid surfaces while sprinting or holding a compatible fishing rod. Added the Water Skiing advancement for sustained travel across water during a block-anchor pull.

## Equipment browsing

- All six Ascendance shield tiers now appear in JEI and the creative inventory.
- Reordered ingredients to place the Ascendance Matrix, raw Latent Ore, Latent Nugget, Latent Ingot, and Latent Block before the Essentium sets. The Raw Latent Ore Block remains under Natural Blocks.

## Updating an existing world

Run `/essence admin balance rebuild` to generate the new fishing rod tier values and Fishing Speed balance for your loaded pack. Existing player progress and human configuration are retained.
