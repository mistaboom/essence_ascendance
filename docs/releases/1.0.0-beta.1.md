# Essence Ascendance 1.0.0-beta.1

The first public beta of Essence Ascendance for **Minecraft 1.21.1**, available
for **Fabric** and **NeoForge**.

## Explore Essence Ascendance

- Dissolve eligible resources into six types of Essence: Offense, Defense,
  Vitality, Mobility, Gathering, and Utility.
- Develop your character through the Ascendance Nexus, category attunement,
  bonuses, and skills.
- Build an Essence network with Crucibles, Pylons, Focuses, and Infusers.
- Infuse Ascendance equipment and progress from Latent toward Transcendent.
- Use the in-game Ascendance Archive for guidance and reference information.
- Explore recipe-based item valuation and procedural progression balancing
  informed by the world's available content.

## Installation

Requires **Java 21**. Choose the jar for your loader and install the mod and its
required dependencies on **both client and server**.

- **Fabric:** Fabric Loader 0.19.3 or newer, Fabric API, and Architectury API
  13.0.11 or newer for Minecraft 1.21.1.
- **NeoForge:** NeoForge 21.1 and Architectury API 13.0.11 or newer for
  Minecraft 1.21.1.
- **JEI is optional** and provides recipe integration.
