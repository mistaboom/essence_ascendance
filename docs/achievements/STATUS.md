# Achievement implementation and traceability

All 69 selected achievements are implemented, covered by executable positive and
negative checks, and exercised by controlled native fixtures on Minecraft 1.21.1
and 26.1.2, Fabric and NeoForge. The final reviewed presentation was accepted by
the user on 2026-11-07. This is not a claim of a human playthrough of all 69.
[ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md) records exact execution scope, counts and archive
hashes, including the distinction between the full gameplay runs and the later
tab-title-only checks.

## Maintained documentation

- [catalog.json](catalog.json): authoritative 69 IDs, exact approved names/actions
  and ID-joined source notes; normal builds require no personal workbook.
- [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md): successful-outcome attribution, rule semantics,
  bounded state, lifecycle, serialization and native administration.
- [presentation.json](presentation.json) and [PRESENTATION.md](PRESENTATION.md):
  display layout, descriptions, frames and existing ItemStack icon provenance.
- [PLAYER-GUIDE.md](PLAYER-GUIDE.md): player-facing behavior and resets.
- [REVIEW-WORLD.md](REVIEW-WORLD.md): reproducible disposable review-world setup.

All completion belongs to native PlayerAdvancements. No achievement gates
Ascension or adds a reward, skill, item or balance effect. ID 22 is the selected
root; legacy Water Skiing is outside the 69 and does not migrate to ID 1.
The tab is **Essence Ascendance**; ID 22 remains **Equivalent Exchange**.
