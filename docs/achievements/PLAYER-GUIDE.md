# Achievement guide

Essence Ascendance has 69 achievements covering progression, machines, combat,
movement, gathering and playful challenges. Open Minecraft's Advancements screen
to browse their names and unlock conditions in the **Essence Ascendance** tab.
**Equivalent Exchange** is its visible entry point; the whole tree can be browsed
before earning it.

Minecraft shows tab buttons only when more than one tab is visible. Vanilla tabs
still unlock normally: obtaining a crafting table in Survival reveals Minecraft's
own tab. An otherwise empty tab strip on a fresh save is expected.

Lines between achievements organize related activities. They are not unlock
requirements: you can earn an achievement before its displayed parent. The tree
adds no Ascension gates, skills, items, experience or other gameplay rewards.
Every completion uses Minecraft's usual toast, with the native sound for challenge
frames. Only **Break Through the Heavens**, **Exodia, Assemble!** and **Limit Break**
are announced in chat. See the [presentation and icon inventory](PRESENTATION.md)
for the complete display map.

## Completing challenges

Unlocks follow your successful Survival actions. A teammate's action, an item
gift, a recipe preview or a failed operation does not replace your required
craft, purchase, upgrade or kill. Reaching an Attunement cap does not prevent
other achievement actions from counting. **Training Montage** specifically
requires positive Attunement earned from three activity types within one category
and chapter.

Equipment requirements mean functional equipment: intact, not fractured or fully
worn out, in its normal armor or hand slot. **Exodia, Assemble!** needs all four
Transcendent armor pieces and a compatible Transcendent main-hand weapon together.
**It's Dangerous to Go Alone** uses a main-hand sword and offhand shield.
**Moon Prism Power!** requires actual night in a dimension with a cycling sky,
not daytime darkness from weather.

Machine credit belongs to the player who established the operation, not whoever
stands nearby. The operator must be online and still allowed to use the machine
when work finishes. **The Factory Must Grow** uses one physical Crucible's supply.
**The Colour of Magic** uses one physical Crucible during one calendar day on the
Overworld clock; sleeping into a new day resets that attempt. **Howl's Moving
Workshop** can use different Infusers and workpieces in the Overworld and Nether.

**Princess Donut’s Bodyguard** keeps the same owned cat for all five kills. The
cat must be within eight blocks at each qualifying kill and must remain unharmed
during and between fights. Damage, death or loss of ownership ends that collection.
Temporary distance does not erase verified kills, but an absent or unloaded cat
cannot supply a qualifying damage-free encounter.

**Under Pressure** needs ore with known natural-generation history. Old or
unobserved ore may not qualify. Newly generated Latent Ore retains that history
through saves and reloads; other observed natural ore loses that evidence when
its chunk unloads or the server restarts. **Terrarian at Heart** needs actual
Nature's Boon rewards in all three dimensions. Packs that disable eligible End
ore or the reward can make that achievement unavailable.

## Progress and resets

Completed achievements survive death, travel, logout and ordinary reloads.
Lifetime collections keep their recorded progress. One-life challenges reset on
actual death; successful death prevention does not count as dying. Short movement,
combat and rod attempts end when their continuity breaks, including logout,
teleportation, changing dimensions or restarting. Separate glides, pulls or sprints
cannot be combined when the description requires one uninterrupted attempt.

**Jumped the Shark** is a single anchored pull crossing at least twenty horizontal
blocks of exposed water. Old **Water Skiing** progress remains compatible with
existing saves, but it is not a seventieth visible selection and does not unlock
Jumped the Shark.

Existing items and world state do not invent past crafts, purchases or kills.
Minecraft's advancement commands can deliberately grant or revoke completions;
they do not fabricate those gameplay actions. Revoking an achievement does not
replay its old action, and a saturated lifetime collection does not automatically
earn it again.
